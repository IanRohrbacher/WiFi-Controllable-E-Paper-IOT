E-Ink Badge

Welcome to my E-Ink Badge! This project took several months to complete. The hardware was originally purchased for a different purpose: A Reusable Digital Notecard for Archery, to display inputted environmental conditions. While I was working on the software I decided to expanded it into a versatile, multi-purpose e-ink display that can be adapted for a variety of applications with minimal effort.

Feel free to draw and please be considerate of others. Enjoy!
